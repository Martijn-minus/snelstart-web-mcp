To the extent permitted by applicable law, the maintainers exclude liability arising from use of the software, including incorrect results, data loss, interruptions, and bookkeeping or filing errors. Mandatory rights and remedies continue to apply.

Use the software with an administration you are authorized to access. Choose the access mode that fits your work. Start with live read-only access or a local offline snapshot if you only need to review records. Make and verify a backup before starting work, especially before allowing writes. Check material results and proposed changes against the source records before posting or filing. Keep local archives secure and decide which records to share with your MCP client.
